Method

The New Metric: True-Livability PSF

The valuation now discounts partially useful and non-livable space instead of treating every square foot as equal.

NLA = Strata Area − (Balcony Area × 0.5) − (A/C Ledge Area × 1.0) True-Livability PSF = Transacted Price ÷ NLA
Why balcony is counted at 50%: it has lifestyle value, but it is not equivalent to air-conditioned indoor space.
Why A/C ledge is counted at 0%: it has no practical interior utility for the buyer.

Buyer Checklist

Before Signing the OTP

Ask the agent for these

  • Exact floor plan for the unit stack and level.
  • Strata title area breakdown if available.
  • Whether balcony, PES, void or sky patio area is included.
  • Recent same-stack transactions, not just project averages.

Red flags

  • Large outdoor space marketed as if it were indoor area.
  • High strata PSF justified only by generic project branding.
  • No comparable 3BR transactions in the same stack or floor band.
