Methodology

Why Strata PSF Misleads — and What We Measure Instead

Singapore's URA records strata area, which includes balconies at 100% of their footprint in your PSF calculation. A 108sqft balcony you use twice a year inflates your apparent square footage the same as a bedroom you sleep in every night. We correct for this.

True Indoor Area = Strata Area − Balcony − AC Ledge − Planter Box True Livability PSF = Transaction Price ÷ True Indoor Area Efficiency % = True Indoor Area ÷ Strata Area × 100 Penalty % = (True Livability PSF − Strata PSF) ÷ Strata PSF × 100

Beyond area, we score four qualitative factors critical to Singapore living: enclosed kitchen (essential for high-heat wok/curry cooking), balcony proportion (lower = better indoor ratio), entrance efficiency (long corridors are wasted strata area you paid for), and MRT walkability (door-to-platform, not "nearby").
